Coso records your lectures and transcribes them on your own device with Whisper — nothing is ever uploaded. Edit speakers and timings, search, and export to TXT, Word, PDF or subtitles.

Everything a lecture needs

100% on-device

Whisper runs on your machine. No uploads, no accounts, works offline once models are cached.

Speakers separated

Diarization tells voices apart, and you can rename “Speaker 2” to “Prof. Rivera” with one click.

Editable everything

Fix a word, a timestamp or a speaker name right in the transcript — exports follow your edits.

Exports that fit

TXT, Markdown, Word, PDF, SRT/VTT subtitles, JSON — or just the audio back.

Noise removal

Neural denoising (DeepFilterNet) cleans up big-hall recordings before transcription.

English & Spanish

The interface and the installer speak your language, and Whisper transcribes dozens more.

Install on Windows

  1. Download the installer above.
  2. Run it — the installer opens in your language.
  3. Launch Coso from the desktop or Start menu.
  4. Pick a Whisper model in Settings (small ones download in seconds).

Install on Android

  1. Download the APK above on your phone.
  2. Allow “install unknown apps” for your browser, once.
  3. Open the APK and confirm the install.
  4. When exporting, the system file picker saves wherever you allow it.

Use it in the browser

  1. Open the web app above — it works on desktop and mobile.
  2. Choose a model the first time; it downloads once and stays cached on your device.
  3. On a phone, use “Add to home screen” to launch it like an app.
Your recordings never leave your device. No telemetry, no cloud, no account — the code is open and you can check.
